# First campaign: identity, membership, and publication policies

**Status:** The policy decisions in this record were reviewed and accepted by decision-maker @Justrebl on 2026-11-13 for issue [#3](https://github.com/CoffeesoftDotDev/MerlAIn/issues/3), including campaign creation and campaign-specific GM/Player role eligibility. The issue remains open until its acceptance criteria are completed; this acceptance does not approve unrelated PRD recommendations.

**Decision-maker:** @Justrebl (the user confirmed this role in this session).

**Scope:** Policies needed for the first manually operated GM/player campaign. This record supplies policy inputs to the contract work; it does not close issue #3 or establish that the technical contracts have been completed.

## Evidence and confirmed boundaries

The [core PRD](../../.copilot-tracking/squad/members/produit/prd/2026-09-28/merlain-prd-v0.1.md) distinguishes user-confirmed scope from proposed target behavior. In its status key, **CONFIRME** is confirmed scope, **CIBLE SPECIFIEE** is testable behavior still subject to review, and **RECOMMANDATION A VALIDER** is not a user decision. In particular:

- The first slice supports local username/email and password or configured OIDC login (FR-CORE-002); it remains usable manually without optional AI (FR-CORE-029).
- Installation capabilities and a person's permissions are separate (FR-CORE-001). Campaign access is scoped to an explicit campaign membership; a module or link alone does not grant access (FR-CORE-004).
- A GM has a private campaign idea notebook (FR-CORE-006). Ideas remain private even when enriched or mentioned by a public scene.
- Campaign chronicles represent scenes/events and can branch and reconverge (FR-CORE-007); narrative elements can be attached to a scene without transferring ownership (FR-CORE-008).
- Human-written shared-journal entries are directly published by a member without prior GM approval (FR-CORE-016). This is distinct from publishing generated material.
- A GM-approved AI version, if the optional capability is used, is tied to a version and audience; it does not expose drafts or secret sources (FR-CORE-023). Search, excerpts, and derivatives must obey campaign and audience access (FR-CORE-024).
- The PRD explicitly leaves R01 (identity, account recovery and bootstrap) and R02 (invitations, roles and membership) as recommendations requiring approval; ownership transfer and last-GM handling are called out as open. Its E01 evidence item requires those policies to be adopted or amended before account-flow design.
- The user confirmed that MerlAIn should be usable by anyone as an open-source solution. Favor self-hostable, provider-neutral defaults and do not require a MerlAIn-hosted service; keep external identity providers optional and configurable. This direction does not select a software license or authorize bundling or redistributing game-rule content.

Issue #3 additionally asks for unambiguous account identity, bootstrap and recovery without mandatory SMTP, explicit OIDC linking, installation/campaign separation, invitations and role assignment, revocation, transfer and last-GM behavior, and rules for private ideas, draft scenes, and publication. Its acceptance criteria also require concrete access examples and a decision-maker. The named decision-maker reviewed this record and said, “Looks good to me, go ahead with implementation.”

## Decisions and alternatives

The decisions marked confirmed or accepted were reviewed by the decision-maker. In a subsequent clarification, the decision-maker confirmed that a user who creates a campaign is its GM and that any platform user can be a GM or Player in a campaign. Technical implementation details remain for the contract work.

| Decision | Status or proposed default | Alternative to decide |
|---|---|---|
| Campaign creation and campaign roles | **Confirmed by @Justrebl on 2026-11-13:** a platform user who creates a campaign becomes its initial GM. GM and Player are campaign-specific roles: any platform user may be a GM in one campaign and a Player in another, and a campaign Player may become a GM through the authorized role-change process. The reviewed proposal retains the creator as initial owner. This grants no installation-administrator authority or access to other campaigns. | None for campaign creation or role eligibility. Contract work must specify the authorized role-change operation consistently with the confirmed policy that an existing campaign GM controls later GM role changes; initial ownership remains part of the technical proposal. |
| Local account identifiers | **Confirmed by @Justrebl on 2026-11-13:** normalize usernames case-insensitively and require uniqueness. Permit email login only when verified and unique; reject ambiguous collisions rather than guessing. A username/email pair always resolves to one account. (R01; AC-CORE-004, 007–008) | Username-only login was not selected. Email changes and collision handling must preserve uniqueness. |
| OIDC identity and linking | **Confirmed by @Justrebl on 2026-11-13:** identify an OIDC identity by issuer/provider plus subject, not email. Link only after the person proves control of both the existing local account and OIDC identity. Never merge accounts, memberships, or roles because emails match. (R01; AC-CORE-005, 007–008) | Separate accounts without linking was not selected; email alone is never sufficient proof. |
| First administrator and recovery | **Bootstrap confirmed by @Justrebl on 2026-11-13:** use a deliberate, one-time setup action; first login, matching email, or OIDC claim never grants administrator rights. **Recovery confirmed:** provide a self-hosted Docker CLI `--admin-reset` for the existing installation administrator only. It shows two separate prompts, each requiring exact `agreed`; any mismatch aborts. It then immediately prompts for a new password without echoing or logging it. It must not depend on SMTP or a hosted reset service, create/replace administrator identities, or change campaign roles, ownership, or data. Password storage/validation remain technical contract details. (R01; AC-CORE-006, 009) | Offline operator provisioning was not selected for initial bootstrap. |
| Installation vs. campaign authority | **Confirmed scope:** an installation administrator manages configuration but gets no automatic campaign membership or content-reading right. Each campaign has its own GM/player memberships. @Justrebl confirmed an audited recovery action may appoint a replacement GM in an archived campaign without exposing its content; this does not grant campaign membership or content access. (FR-CORE-001, 004; R02) | None for the approved archived-campaign recovery action. |
| Invitation and initial role | **Confirmed by @Justrebl on 2026-11-13:** use a transferable campaign invitation link that expires after seven days; redemption by an authenticated account grants membership and is the acceptance action. Invitation redemption grants the Player role; an existing campaign GM may later change a member's role to GM. No module activation or account creation alone grants membership. (R02; AC-CORE-010–011) | Named-account-only invitations, separate confirmation, GM-role links, and GM-selected expiry were not selected. |
| Revocation | **Confirmed by @Justrebl on 2026-11-13:** any current GM in the campaign may revoke a member's access to that campaign. Requests already in progress may finish; requests started afterward are denied immediately, including through existing sessions, saved links, search, or exports. Revocation does not delete campaign content or recall downloaded copies. An installation administrator without campaign membership has no revocation authority. (AC-CORE-011–012; PRD §6) | Immediate denial of already-running requests was not selected. |
| Ownership transfer and last GM | **Confirmed by @Justrebl on 2026-11-13:** the current owner may nominate an existing GM; ownership changes only after that GM accepts, with other memberships unchanged. If the last GM leaves, is removed, or loses GM status before a successor accepts, the campaign is archived rather than blocking the change. Data is retained; current players keep read-only access to content already published to them, while private ideas and drafts stay hidden. An installation administrator may appoint a replacement GM through an audited recovery action without reading campaign content. (R02; issue #3) | Blocking the last GM's change until a successor accepts was not selected. |
| Private work and scene publication | **Accepted by @Justrebl on 2026-11-13:** only a current campaign GM may publish a prepared scene. The GM selects a subset of current player members; “all current players” is a convenience that snapshots those members at publication time so later invitees gain no retroactive access. Edits require explicit republishing as a new version; player views show only the current published version. If a source/right changes or disclosure is suspected, suspend the affected projection until GM review. Private ideas, drafts, and sources are never exposed by reference. (FR-CORE-006–008, 023–024; AC-CORE-016–018, 067–071) | Publishing by any member, dynamic audience membership, and automatic updates to player views were not selected. |
| Human journal vs. prepared scene | **Confirmed scope:** a member's human-written shared-journal entry is directly visible to authorized participants without prior GM approval. Any moderation is a separate, visible post-publication action. This does not permit publication of GM notes or scene drafts. (FR-CORE-016–017; AC-CORE-046, 049) | Any change would revise confirmed PRD scope and requires explicit scope approval; no change is proposed. |

## Actor / action / content examples

The following outcomes illustrate the accepted policies; none of these behaviors is implemented by this document.

| Actor and action | Content or boundary | Outcome and reason |
|---|---|---|
| Authenticated platform user creates a new campaign | Initial campaign ownership and role | **Allow:** create the campaign and make the creator its GM, without granting roles in other campaigns or installation-administrator authority. The reviewed proposal also makes the creator its initial owner. |
| A campaign Player is made a GM by an existing GM | Campaign-specific role assignment | **Allow:** the Player becomes a GM in that campaign. This does not change their role in any other campaign. |
| Installation administrator enables a configured login method | Installation configuration | **Allow** configuration management; it does not grant campaign membership. |
| Installation administrator opens a campaign's private idea or draft scene | Campaign content without membership | **Deny**. Administrator status alone is not a campaign role. |
| GM in Campaign A, with no membership in Campaign B, opens a private note in Campaign B | Cross-campaign content | **Deny**, even if the GM knows a saved URL. If the same account has an explicit Campaign B membership, only that campaign-specific role applies. |
| GM creates a transferable invitation link for Campaign A | Campaign membership | **Confirmed:** allow redemption by an authenticated account during the seven-day validity period; redemption accepts the invitation and grants Player membership. |
| Campaign A player uses an old bookmark after revocation | Campaign resource through a saved link or existing session | **Deny** subsequent reads and writes, including search/export. Already downloaded copies cannot be recalled by the application. |
| GM prepares a scene that refers to a private idea | Draft scene and its private source | **Allow** preparation for the GM; **deny** player access to the draft, idea, and private excerpt. A reference does not publish its source. |
| GM publishes one reviewed scene version to selected players | Published scene version | **Allow** only those players to read that version. **Deny** access to its draft, alternate versions, private sources, and any other campaign's material. |
| A source or audience right changes after publication | Previously published scene | Suspend the affected projection until a GM reviews it. |
| Player publishes their own human journal entry | Shared campaign journal | **Allow** direct publication to authorized participants without prior GM approval, consistent with confirmed FR-CORE-016. Subsequent moderation is a separate proposed policy. |
| Last GM leaves, is removed, or loses GM status before a successor accepts | Campaign membership and ownership | **Confirmed:** archive the campaign rather than block the change. Retain its data; players keep read-only access only to content already published to them, and private ideas/drafts remain hidden. An installation administrator may appoint a replacement GM through the audited recovery action without reading content. |
